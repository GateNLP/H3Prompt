This service gives access to a narrative classifier which aims to classify news articles into a pre-defined two-level taxonomy of main narratives and sub-narratives across multiple languages. We propose Hierarchical Three-Step Prompting (H3Prompt) for multilingual narrative classification.

Our methodology follows a three-step Large Language Model (LLM) prompting strategy, where the model first categorises an article into one of two domains (Ukraine-Russia War or Climate Change), then identifies the most relevant main narratives, and finally assigns sub-narratives. Our approach secured the top position on the SemEval 2025 Task 10 Subtask 2, English language test set.

Default annotations
:Narrative An annotation which spans the entire document and contains features describing the classification.
  • category, the main domain of the document. For this classifier this will always be either "Ukraine-Russia War", "Climate Change", or for documents unrelated to either domain it will be "Other".
  • main_narrative, the title of the main narrative
  • sub_narrative, the title of the sub-narrative
